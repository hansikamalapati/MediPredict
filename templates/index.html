How MediPredict Works

01. Historical Usage

The system uses historical medicine consumption records to understand usage patterns.

02. Demand Prediction

A Random Forest machine-learning model predicts the expected daily medicine demand.

03. Stockout Prevention

Predicted demand is compared with current stock and supplier lead time to identify stockout risk and recommend replenishment.

HOW IT WORKS

Stockout Prediction Logic

1

Analyze Usage

The system calculates average daily medicine consumption using the latest 30 days of usage history.

2

Estimate Stock Duration

Current inventory is divided by average daily usage to estimate how many days the medicine will last.

3

Compare Delivery Time

Estimated stock duration is compared with the supplier's delivery lead time.

4

Recommend Reorder

The system calculates a recommended reorder quantity to maintain safety stock.

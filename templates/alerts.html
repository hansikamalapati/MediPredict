How Alerts Are Generated

Predicted Demand

The ML model estimates expected daily consumption.

Stock Coverage

Current stock is divided by predicted daily demand to estimate remaining days.

Supplier Lead Time

The system compares stock coverage with the expected supplier delivery time.

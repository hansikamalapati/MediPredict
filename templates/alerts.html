How Alerts Are Generated

Predicted Demand

The ML model estimates the expected daily consumption of each medicine.

Stock Coverage

Current stock is divided by predicted daily demand to estimate how many days the available stock can last.

Supplier Lead Time

The system compares stock coverage with the expected supplier delivery time to identify possible stockouts.
